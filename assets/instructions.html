Instructions per sound level meter

Bruel and kjaar 2250, 2270, 01db-fusion and svantek959 are supported

Bruel and Kjaer 2250 or 2270

Export from BZ5503

Choose ASCII export (.txt), tab-separated. You need the log files of the broadband and the spectra (2 files) The optional wav-files are hidden in the folder structure of the project and you need to copy them separately drag and drop the 2 files in the upload component

Svantek959

General settings on SLM:

Menu - Measurement function: - 1/3 octave

Menu - Input - Measurement setup: start delay: 0s; integr. period: 1s; rep. cycles: inf; logger: ON

audio file settings: !!!PUT USB STICK IN SVAN 959!!! - Menu - Setup - USB-HOST PORT, Wave recording: PCM, 48 kHz, bits per sample 24

Export from SVANPC++

Choose file, export, export data, ASCII export with comments and no word wrap (.txt)

01dB - Fusion

General settings on SLM:

Make sure the option is selected that the (.csv) files are saved on the SD-card.
